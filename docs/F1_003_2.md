## Scope and evidence

Awarded race and sprint points are summed with Decimal; compare each snapshot with the preceding one. This is a separate computation over the same F1DB data, NOT independent verification of every historical result or a sporting-regulation scoring engine. Points deductions already included in standings are not applied twice. Known discrepancy patterns are accepted only for exact amounts and contexts (2007 exclusion is checked against final exclusion). 2018 aggregation is by constructor, with the verified entrant split required for the reset exception; entrant-by-entrant arithmetic still pending. Historical snapshots for 2007 are retrospective, not standings as known on the original race date. Sources: [2000 McLaren](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2000/team/McLaren-Mercedes), [2007 teams](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2007/team), [2018 entrant change](https://api.fia.com/news/fia-approves-mid-season-entry-racing-point-force-india), [2020 Racing Point](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2020/team/Racing-Point-BWT-Mercedes). No historical corrections are added by this audit.

Checks count race/qualifying leaders, podium places, presence of fastest-lap records, session and snapshot coverage and wins. Qualifying leader is not necessarily actual grid pole. Equal-rank/equal-points groups are preserved as diagnostics (not automatically errors); unequal points sharing a rank are flagged. Countback uses classified race finishes only, not the full historical regulatory rules; candidates require review, not automatic rank changes. 2021 Belgium has no fastest-lap result in the official results table: https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2021/races/1074/belgium/fastest-laps. Do not fabricate one. No validation of fastest-lap times or independent rule tables yet.

Crosswalk classifier groups reported fields; only [time] vs [time,null,null] is a recognized single-session format match. Missing values, positions, and 1ms differences remain diagnostic. No aliases approved and no predictions migrated. For 2000, 333 raw differences: 143 classification, 90 missing time, 87 format, 12 1ms, 1 conflict. Unmatched record: Nick Heidfeld R06 race.
